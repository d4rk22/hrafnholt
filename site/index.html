Make it yours.

Run Hrafnholt with Docker, copy the starter settings, and connect your first service. The setup guide walks you through each step.

Open the setup guide

You stay in control.
Hrafnholt reads your services. It doesn’t change their settings or start jobs.

Your dashboard stays yours.
Host it on your own equipment. Connections use a settings file; there’s no setup wizard yet.

Keep live data private.
There’s no built-in login. Use authentication and HTTPS when sharing your live dashboard. Security guide
