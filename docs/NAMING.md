# Naming review

This is a risk record, not legal advice or a trademark clearance opinion.

## Current decision

**Hrafnholt** is the project name, adopted by explicit maintainer decision on
2026-08-17. It is an ordinary Old Norse compound — *hrafn* (raven) and *holt*
(wood, wooded hill) — chosen to preserve the meaning of the project's former
name while removing any association with modern fiction or its rights
holders. Old Norse vocabulary carries no estate, and the compound is not a
famous name, place, brand, or product.

At adoption time the name was checked and found unused across GitHub
(repositories, users, and organizations), npm, PyPI, Docker Hub, and the
.com/.net/.org/.io TLDs (.dev/.app returned NXDOMAIN but their registry
whois did not answer). No collision with existing software, and no known
commercial user of the name, was found. No formal trademark-database search
was performed; the maintainer accepts that residual risk as proportionate to
a non-commercial hobby project, with a later rename as the accepted remedy
should a conflict ever surface.

## History

The project was previously named after a location in J.R.R. Tolkien's
fiction. That name was retained provisionally through the open-source
extraction while direct Tolkien references were removed from the
application: the former storage nickname, mountain/gate filenames and
artwork, and the 24-name privacy alias list. Public mode now defaults to a
manually selected list of 24 Argonaut names from the ancient Greek sources
(Apollonius of Rhodes, Apollodorus), which are in the public domain;
operators may configure their own roster via `presentation.privacy.aliases`.
The storage panel uses an original mountain-gate SVG.

Private operational history may still describe the former names because it
is historical evidence; it is not application documentation or a release
input.

## Standing guidance

Do not add character, place, title, quotation, or artwork themes from any
modern fictional work to the application or its release branding. Motifs
drawn from mythology, folklore, and other public-domain sources are
acceptable and are this project's house style.

Status: **resolved — Hrafnholt adopted by maintainer decision, residual
risk accepted.**
